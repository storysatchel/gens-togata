# {Adventure Title}

*One-line pitch. Tone & scale (e.g. "Gritty urban one-shot, 3–4 hours").*

## Premise

- **Situation:** What is happening, and why now.
- **Stakes:** What goes wrong if no one intervenes.
- **Hook:** Why the player characters get pulled in.

## Arc

*The session's dramatic spine — plot the beats, improvise between them.*

- **Opening pressure:** The situation as players first meet it; what forces them to act.
- **Escalation:** How the stakes ratchet; a clock the GM can tick when players stall.
- **Turn:** The midpoint reversal or revelation that recontextualizes the goal.
- **Climax:** The confrontation the session drives toward; the choice that resolves the stakes.
- **Resolution:** What a win, a loss, and a costly win each look like.

## NPCs

### {NPC Name}
- **Role:** Their place in the situation.
- **Wants:** Their driving goal.
- **Stance:** Sympathetic, antagonistic, or ambiguous.
- **Secret:** A complication or truth players can uncover.
- **Voice:** One line to improvise their manner from.
- **Visual.** Hair, face, build, style, key items of dress. **Cast:** 1–2 actors who'd play them.
- **Register.** Characteristic expression at introduction, if fixed (one emotion + its tell). Optional.
